Exhibit A · My best work

Can a cheaper foam protect just as well?

Sirajul Foam Industries, Chittagong, 2024. Scroll through it in four steps.

Cushion curveIllustrative
$$$Starting material cost
Cushioning polymer qualification, 2024PE, PVC and EVA foam · ASTM and ISTA test methods
step 1

The problem

The protective foam cost too much. But any cheaper material still had to survive real shipping: drops, vibration and heat.

step 2

Set the limit

Every product has a fragility limit, the most shock it can take. Anything above that line breaks it. The target zone is below it.

step 3

Test and pick

I ran side-by-side cushioning, vibration and thermal tests on PE, PVC and EVA using ASTM and ISTA methods, and worked with suppliers on alternates. One foam stayed in the zone with less material.

step 4

The result

−25%

Material cost, with no loss in protection.
